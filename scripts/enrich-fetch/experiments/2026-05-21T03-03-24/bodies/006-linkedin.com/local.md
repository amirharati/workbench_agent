3️⃣ There are at least 3 reasons why I believe you should learn causality in 2023. Here's my take 👇 ⭕ The number of Arxiv research papers on causality grows exponentially year-to-year, showing that many talented people invest their career resources in the topic. One of the early outcomes of this trend is that we had two (!) causal workshops at NeurIPS 2022. ⭕ Causality-aware data scientists can bring more value to the business, even when working on non-causal projects. Their awareness of how structural biases can harm generalization makes them more efficient and more effective in spotting problems at early stages of modeling, which saves a lot of money and time. ⭕ Big players like BMW, Amazon and Microsoft invest in research on causality and its applications. But that's not all. A significant part of my speaking engagements last year was about causality, in particular in the context of automotive industry. This shows that there's a strong interest in the topic and that the industry incrementally puts more weight on these skills. To summarize, more and more high quality talent invests their careers in causality research.
