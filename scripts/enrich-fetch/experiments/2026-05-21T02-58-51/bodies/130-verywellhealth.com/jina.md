### Key Takeaways

*   CBD may help reduce stress and anxiety, which are known to contribute to erectile dysfunction.
*   There is no current research evidence supporting CBD as a direct treatment for erectile dysfunction.

Cannabidiol (CBD) may offer indirect benefits for erectile dysfunction (ED) by alleviating stress and anxiety, which can impact sexual performance. However, there is no research evidence supporting CBD as a direct treatment for ED.

## What Is Erectile Dysfunction?

Erectile dysfunction is defined as the inability to achieve or maintain an erection more than 75% of the time.1 The condition affects 52% of males between ages 40 and 70.1 It can be frustrating and impair the quality of life of those who experience it.

ED can be triggered by factors such as hormonal changes, high blood pressure, medication side effects, stress, or psychological concerns. Although ED can occur at any age, people over 50 are at a higher risk.1

Certain health conditions can raise your risk of ED, such as:1

*   Diabetes
*   Heart disease
*   High cholesterol
*   Sleep disorders
*   Alcoholism
*   Obesity

## What Is Cannabidiol (CBD)?

Cannabidiol (CBD) is one of over 85 active chemicals found in the cannabis plant. Unlike the compound [tetrahydrocannabinol (THC)](https://www.verywellhealth.com/the-difference-between-cbd-and-thc-8621404), CBD is not psychoactive (meaning it does not make you "high.")2

CBD acts on the [endocannabinoid system,](https://www.verywellhealth.com/the-difference-between-cbd-and-thc-8621404) a network of cellular receptors densely packed throughout the brain and body. The system helps regulate sleep, pain, memory, body temperature, digestion, emotional processing, and immune function, among other things.3

By binding to these receptors and blocking certain chemical signals, CBD is thought to reduce anxiety, improve sleep, enhance mood, and control chronic pain.3

There are three different types of CBD that can act on the body in different ways:4

*   **Full-spectrum CBD oil**: This contains many of the cannabinoids and compounds of the cannabis plant, including up to 0.3% THC.
*   **Broad-spectrum CBD oil**: This contains all the same compounds as full-spectrum CBD, minus THC.
*   **Isolate CBD**: This only contains CBD and is often found in crystal or powder form.

### Is CBD Legal?

Purchasing CBD is federally legal as long as it doesn't contain more than 0.3% THC. With that said, there are no other FDA-approved drug products that contain CBD.5

## Benefits of CBD Oil for ED

There is currently no research supporting the benefits of CBD for treating ED. One recent review published in the _Journal of Applied Toxicology_ even suggested that the long-term use of CBD can impair male sexual function by reducing the concentration of hormones that influence the sexual response.6

### Indirect Benefits

The ability to achieve and sustain an erection is due to a combination of physiological, psychological, and emotional responses to sexual stimulation. While there is no evidence that CBD has any physical impact on ED, it may alleviate some of the emotional stresses that contribute to sexual dysfunction.

Stress, anxiety, and depression are all known to reduce libido (sex drive) which, in turn, increases the risk of ED. In up to 40% of cases, ED is thought to be [psychogenic](https://www.verywellhealth.com/psychogenic-erectile-dysfunction-5201654), meaning that it is primarily or entirely due to psychological factors.7 For these individuals especially, CBD may help,

CBD has been shown to reduce stress and anxiety in multiple studies. It also shows potential for minimizing depression, performance anxiety around public speaking, and social anxiety.8

CBD has also been shown to lower blood pressure after a single oral dose.9 While the effect is short-lived, it can potentially increase the flow of blood to the penis at the time of sex.

Whether any of these effects correspond to improvements in sexual function remains under debate. Further research is needed.

## Are There Any Side Effects?

Overall, using CBD oil is considered generally safe. Some people may experience side effects like:10

*   Dry mouth
*   Dizziness
*   Fatigue
*   Decreased appetite
*   Diarrhea

These effects tend to be mild and transient, but call your healthcare provider if they persist or worsen.

### Interactions and Contraindications

There are many drugs that can interact with CBD. Some can become less effective or cause greater side effects when taken together.

Chief among these are:11

*   Blood thinners like [Jantoven (warfarin)](https://www.verywellhealth.com/blood-thinners-5212486)
*   Thyroid medications like [Synthroid (levothyroxine)](https://www.verywellhealth.com/levothyroxine-brand-name-vs-generic-versions-1124055)
*   Antidepressants
*   Antiseizure medications (ASMs)
*   [Heart arrhythmia drugs](https://www.verywellhealth.com/heart-arrhythmia-medication-8693033)

There are few absolute contraindications to the use of CBD oil. With that said, people with a known [sesame allergy](https://www.verywellhealth.com/sesame-seed-allergy-83182) should avoid CBD as it has a similar allergen protein structure.4

Individuals with serious mental illness, like schizophrenia or bipolar disorder, should also give CBD a miss (especially full-spectrum CBD oil which contains THC),4

### ED and Cannabis Smokers

Smoking cannabis has a decidedly more adverse impact on erectile dysfunction when compared to CBD. A 2019 review of studies involving 3,395 healthy adults found that the incidence of ED was twice as high among cannabis smokers than non-smokers.12

## Dosage

There currently is no recommended dosage for CBD oil. The effects vary from person to person, and it may take some experimentation to find a dose that is effective for you. Before trying CBD for ED or any other health purpose, contact your healthcare provider to ensure that it is safe and does not interact with any medicines you take.

## How Do You Use CBD for ED?

CBD comes in many different forms, including extracts, tinctures, capsules, soft gels, chewing gums, vape cartridges, and topical creams or ointments,

CBD products can be readily sourced at:

*   Online retailers
*   Drugstores and pharmacy
*   Health and wellness shops
*   Marijuana or CBD dispensaries

There are different ways that you can use CBD oil if you have ED.

You can take CBD oil by mouth before sex to reduce performance anxiety and improve blood flow. Never exceed the manufacturer's recommended dose on the product label.

[CBD oils](https://www.verywellhealth.com/cbd-oil-for-diabetes-5113061) can be applied to the penis for masturbation (which some people believe can enhance sexual pleasure).13 Unless sold as a sexual lubricant, they should not be used for anal or vaginal sex as the [carrier oils](https://www.verywellhealth.com/cbd-oil-for-diabetes-5113061) may be irritating. There are an increasing number of CBD-infused lubricants you can buy instead.

When used for therapeutic purposes, an oral broad-spectrum or full-spectrum CBD oil is recommended as it has greater bioavailability (meaning a higher concentration of the active ingredient in the bloodstream after you take it).4

## Other Treatment Options

If you have ED, there are more proven methods of treatment you should consider. These include:1

*   [**PDE5 inhibitors**](https://www.verywellhealth.com/cialis-vs-viagra-effectiveness-dosage-and-safety-7091128): These are oral drugs like Viagra (sildenafil) and Cialis (tadalafil) used in the first-line treatment of ED.
*   [**Alprostadil**](https://www.verywellhealth.com/erectile-dysfunction-injections-what-you-need-to-know-5201541): These are drugs you can inject into the penis and use as a rectal suppository to increase blood flow to the penis.
*   [**Testosterone replacement**](https://www.verywellhealth.com/testosterone-replacement-therapy-4581254): This is delivered by injection, patch, gel, or subcutaneous pellet if testosterone levels are abnormally low.
*   [**Penis pump**](https://www.verywellhealth.com/penis-pumps-for-ed-5210450): This is a canister-like device placed over the penis that draws blood into your penis using a manual or mechanical vacuum pump.
*   [**Penile implant**](https://www.verywellhealth.com/penile-implant-8622866): This is a surgical procedure in which an inflatable tube or a malleable rod is implanted in the penis to enable intercourse.

There are a number of lifestyle changes you can make to improve ED. These include exercising regularly, managing stress with activities like yoga and meditation, eating a healthy diet, and creating space to take care of yourself.
