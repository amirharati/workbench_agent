## Your Research Lives Here, Permanently

ChatGPT resets between sessions. NotebookLM caps at 50 sources. Otio is the workspace where research accumulates and compounds over weeks and months.

![Image 16](https://framerusercontent.com/images/6rp2UnPYgmuRjgdSbrWhKDNk0.png?width=1448&height=1086)

### No Session Resets, No File Caps, No Lost Work

Upload 60 papers in January. Add 30 client documents in March. Ask questions across all 90 sources in May. Everything stays in your Otio workspace, searchable and answerable. ChatGPT forgets your sources when the session ends. NotebookLM caps at 50 notebooks. Otio has no limits. Your research builds, it does not reset.

### Search Across Everything You've Ever Uploaded

Remember that podcast episode from six weeks ago that mentioned a specific case study? Search your Otio library and find it in seconds. Your workspace becomes a permanent research archive. Every source you've ever uploaded is indexed, searchable, and ready to answer questions.

### Build Research Projects That Span Weeks and Months

A thesis takes months. A legal case takes weeks. A market analysis evolves as new data arrives. Otio is built for research that accumulates over time, not one-off questions. Add sources as you find them. Refine your questions as your understanding deepens. Export when ready.

### Organize by Project, Not by Session

Create separate workspaces for separate projects. Keep your thesis research separate from your client work. Each workspace maintains its own source library, conversation history, and outputs. Switch between projects without losing context or mixing sources.

## From Reading Backlog to Structured Starting Point

The blank page is not the problem. The problem is the 60 unread papers, the 30 client documents you have not synthesised, and the 10 hours of video you need to extract from before you can start writing.

### Research Is a Workflow Problem, Not a Writing Problem

Otio is built for the upstream work. Upload everything, ask Otio to pull the key findings and themes, and start writing from a structured synthesis instead of a blank page.

![Image 18](https://framerusercontent.com/images/96uaNoh48Gw13DxfMGzdO7KTPc.png?width=1536&height=1024)

### Pull Key Findings Across 60 Sources in Minutes

Upload 60 research papers. Ask Otio to pull the three most common methodologies, the five key findings, and the two biggest contradictions across all of them. What used to take a weekend of reading and note-taking now takes 10 minutes. You still do the thinking. Otio just gets you there faster.

### Group Arguments by Theme, Not by Source

Upload 50 articles on climate policy. Ask Otio to group the arguments by theme: economic impact, regulatory frameworks, technological solutions. Instead of reading 50 articles sequentially and trying to remember which one said what, you get a thematic synthesis with citations back to the original sources.

### Surface Contradictions and Gaps Before You Write

Ask Otio to identify where your sources disagree. Find the gaps in the literature before you start drafting, not after your advisor points them out. This is the kind of synthesis that used to require multiple read-throughs and a spreadsheet. Otio surfaces it in one query, with citations to the exact passages.
