## How to choose the right refurbished iPhone

All models mentioned below are available above based on real-time inventory. Use the filters to quickly find the iPhone that matches your needs and budget.

### Photography & video

If camera quality is your priority, look at Pro and Pro Max models. These versions offer advanced sensors, better low-light processing, and features like improved optical zoom and Cinematic mode. The **iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro, and their Pro Max variants** are the top picks for mobile photography enthusiasts.

### Battery life & everyday use

For heavy daily use (browsing, streaming, social media, email), Plus and Pro Max models stand out thanks to their larger batteries.

The iPhone 14 Plus and iPhone 15 Plus deliver excellent all-day [battery life](https://secondcell.ca/en/blogs/secondcell/telephones-meilleure-batterie-autonomie) at a more accessible price point than Pro versions. These devices easily last a full day without needing a charge.

### Budget-friendly options

Previous-generation models like the iPhone 11, iPhone 12, or standard iPhone 13 let you access the Apple ecosystem without breaking the bank. These devices remain capable for everyday tasks and continue receiving [iOS updates](https://secondcell.ca/en/blogs/secondcell/combien-temps-cellulaire-recevra-mise-a-jour). They're the smart choice if you want a reliable iPhone without stretching your budget.

### Compact size (Mini / SE)

Prefer a phone that fits comfortably in one hand or pocket? The iPhone 13 Mini packs full 13-series power into a smaller form factor. The iPhone SE is another solid option for those who appreciate the classic design with Touch ID. These models are perfect for users who prioritize portability.

### Storage size (64 / 128 / 256 GB)

64 GB works if you primarily store photos in the cloud and don't install many apps. 128 GB is the balanced choice for most users. 256 GB or higher is best if you keep lots of photos, videos, or large games directly on your device.
