## Frequently Asked Questions

### What forms of payment do you accept?

We are powered by Stripe, which accepts all major debit and credit card payments (Visa, American Express, Mastercard), as well as other local payment solutions.

### Will I be charged any sales-tax?

tabExtend covers the local sales tax on our end, so you won't need to pay any additional sales tax. Team plans exclude VAT.

### Do you have a affiliate program?

Yes, earn a 25% commission on the revenue from everyone you refer to tabExtend through our affiliate program. Join us today and learn more at [tabextend.getrewardful.com](https://tabextend.getrewardful.com/)

### Who stores my credit card number?

All payments and security measures are managed by Stripe. We never have access to or handle your credit card details. Stripe is PCI-certified, and card numbers are encrypted using AES-256. Learn more at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com/)

### What is your refund policy?

Should you wish to cancel your plan for any reason, we offer a full refund within 30 days.

### Do you offer discounts to students or teachers?

Yes, if you are a student or a teacher and you want a discount please contact us and we will set you up.
