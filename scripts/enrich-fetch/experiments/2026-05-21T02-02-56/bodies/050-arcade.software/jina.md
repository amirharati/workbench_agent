#### Frequently asked questions

Do you have special pricing for startups?

![Image 279](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61d72a2cda50bc679e28766b/65d871a48c6fe0c94ed5eeb0_Caret-Down.svg)

Yes! We're happy to offer discounts on the Growth or Pro plan for companies before their Series A or smaller. Just reach out to us on Intercom and tell us about your company.

Will my billing change when new features are added?

![Image 280](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61d72a2cda50bc679e28766b/65d871a48c6fe0c94ed5eeb0_Caret-Down.svg)

Nope! Your billing stays the same no matter how many awesome features we add.

Can I unsubscribe anytime?

![Image 281](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61d72a2cda50bc679e28766b/65d871a48c6fe0c94ed5eeb0_Caret-Down.svg)

For sure. Any time you want to unsubscribe, you can select the Free plan from the billing page. You'll maintain access to features until your next billing period. (For example, if you subscribe on the 10th and unsubscribe on the 11th, you'll have access to all features of your plan until the 10th of the next month.) When your plan moves to Free, you'll loose access to premium features, Insights, and being able to edit more than three Arcades.

Can I add additional seats on my plan?

![Image 282](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61d72a2cda50bc679e28766b/65d871a48c6fe0c94ed5eeb0_Caret-Down.svg)

- If you are on the Growth Plan you can add additional seats for $150/month. Go to Billing in your Arcade Settings.

- If you are on the Enterprise plan, you can add more seats anytime. Please speak with your CSM for additional information.

- The Free and Pro plans are limited to 1 seat each.

Can I add additional AI credits?

![Image 283](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61d72a2cda50bc679e28766b/65d871a48c6fe0c94ed5eeb0_Caret-Down.svg)

- If you are on the Growth Plan you can top-up AI credits anytime. Go to Billing in your Arcade Settings.

- If you are on the Enterprise plan, please speak with your CSM for additional AI credits.

- Additional AI credits is not available on the Free and Pro plans.
